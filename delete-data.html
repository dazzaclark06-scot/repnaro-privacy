Repnaro — Data deletion

23 November 2026 · Android phone and Wear OS companion · com.repnaro.app

Repnaro does not require an account or store workout and nutrition history in a Repnaro-hosted cloud database. Your phone and watch store their own local app data. You can delete local data yourself and request deletion of information you have sent to Repnaro support.

Delete Repnaro data on your phone

  1. Open Repnaro on your Android phone.
  2. Go to Settings → Data Management → Factory reset Repnaro.
  3. Follow the confirmation prompt and enter RESET to erase Repnaro's local phone records and local recovery/safety copies.

This removes the Repnaro records stored by the phone app, including profile information, training history, measurements, nutrition preferences and meal plans. It does not delete records held by other apps, a paired watch or files you exported elsewhere.

Delete data on a paired Galaxy Watch

Phone data deletion does not automatically erase data held only on the watch. To remove the watch's Repnaro records, use your watch's Wear OS app settings to clear Repnaro's storage or uninstall Repnaro from the watch. If the phone and watch were previously synced, records may still exist on the other device until separately deleted there.

Delete exported backups and other copies

If you exported an encrypted or older plain-JSON backup, delete the file from the location you chose, including Downloads, removable storage or a cloud drive. Also check that provider's trash and version history. Repnaro cannot remotely delete those files. Health Connect and Samsung Health keep their own records; use those services' deletion controls separately.

Request deletion of support correspondence or tester details

Email dazzaclark06@gmail.com with the subject Repnaro data deletion request. Specify whether you want deletion of support emails and attachments you sent, removal from the invited Google Play tester list, or both. Send the request from the email address concerned so the publisher can identify the records. Do not email health records, backup files or passwords.

Repnaro can act on correspondence and tester-list information under its control. It cannot remotely access or delete records stored only on your devices or within third-party services. Your email provider, Google Play and other third parties may have their own retention or deletion policies.

What is retained, and for how long?

  • Local phone and watch data: stays on the respective device until you delete it or remove its app storage.
  • Exported backups: remain wherever you saved them until you or the storage provider delete them; provider trash and backups may persist according to provider policy.
  • Support correspondence and tester-list entries: can be requested for deletion using the email above. No fixed deletion timeframe is promised; the publisher will respond to requests and may retain information where legally required.
  • Health Connect / Samsung Health records: managed separately by those services, not deleted by resetting Repnaro.

For additional details, see the Repnaro privacy policy.
