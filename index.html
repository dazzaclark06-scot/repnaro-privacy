Repnaro privacy policy

21 November 2026 · Android phone and Wear OS companion · com.repnaro.app

This policy covers Repnaro’s fitness and meal-planning apps. The publisher and developer is Repnaro. For app support or privacy requests, including deletion of correspondence you have sent us, email dazzaclark06@gmail.com.

Your profile, training and meals

You can enter a name, age, sex, measurements, training goals, schedule, available equipment and dietary preferences. Repnaro uses them on the phone to plan training and meals. It saves workouts, drafts, exercise names, dates, sets, repetitions, loads, rest times, intervals, progression, measurements, nutrition targets, meal plans, swaps, custom meals and shopping lists. The recipe and exercise photographs are supplied with the app. You do not need a Repnaro account.

Health Connect on your phone

On Android 14 or newer, Settings → Health Connect → Import workouts lets you read exercise sessions from the previous 30 days. Repnaro reads the source, record identifier, activity, title where available, and start and end times to display sessions and recognise repeat imports. Optional access to total calories burned, distance and heart rate adds available same-source metrics to the workout. Running pace uses elapsed time. Samsung Health data can appear if Samsung Health writes it to Health Connect. Repnaro neither writes records to Health Connect nor imports them automatically in the background.

You choose whether to grant access or import. Manual logging works without an import. Revoke access in Android Health Connect settings at any time. Revocation stops future reads. Previously imported records can still be present in Repnaro until you remove them.

Your watch and paired phone

The watch stores workouts, entered weights and repetitions, timing, pending events and pairing information. For a sensor workout you explicitly start, Wear OS Health Services can supply heart-rate samples or summaries and estimated energy when the hardware and permissions allow. Body Sensors supports heart rate and Physical Activity supports energy estimates. Repnaro does not request a GPS route. Denying a permission can prevent a sensor workout from starting.

Pairing allows Repnaro to send workout libraries, routines and targets to your watch and return completed sets, timings and available measurements to your phone. The apps keep acknowledgements and retry queues so the pair can reconnect. Google Play services provides the Wear OS Data Layer. Nearby traffic uses encrypted Bluetooth. When devices use the Google cloud relay, Google documents end-to-end encryption of the message content. Repnaro authenticates paired messages. Device and installation identifiers used for reliable delivery differ from advertising IDs.

Where information goes

Repnaro stores records in Android app-private storage. Android’s device protection applies. Ordinary app records do not have a separate password-encryption layer. Automatic Android app backup is disabled. There is no Repnaro-hosted health database, advertising, account, analytics or crash-reporting service built into these apps.

Portable backups and restore

In Settings → Backup & Recovery you can export a portable backup. Repnaro encrypts it with your recovery password before writing it to the destination you choose in Android’s document picker. That destination can be on the device, removable media or a cloud provider. The provider can see file metadata. Keep the password separately. Repnaro cannot recover it. Backups can include profile, workout, nutrition, measurement and pending recovery records from the phone. Pairing credentials and records present only on a disconnected watch are excluded. Previously created plain JSON backups remain readable by anyone who has them.

Restore verifies the backup and asks for confirmation before replacing supported records. Use the date and data summary to identify the copy you want. The app can retain a local safety copy during recovery. Google Play, Google Play services, your device software, Health Connect, the website host and any file provider you choose operate under their respective privacy policies. Opening the hosted policy sends normal web-request information such as your IP address to the host. Reading the bundled copy inside the app works offline.

Retention, deletion and security

If you email us, the publisher and its email provider receive your address, message and any attachments you send. We use them to handle your request. If you join a private test, your invitation address is used to manage access through Google Play. Please keep health records, backup files and recovery passwords out of support messages. You can ask us to remove your correspondence or remove you from our tester list.

Training records stay on the device until you remove them. In Settings → Data Management → Factory reset Repnaro, confirm RESET to erase Repnaro’s local phone records and safety copies. Handle the watch’s app storage separately. External backup files, provider trash or version history and original records held by Health Connect or Samsung Health each have their own deletion controls. Data already synced can remain on the other device.

Your choices, contact and changes

For questions about your information, contact the publisher using the route above. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or erase information the publisher holds and to raise a concern with your local data protection authority. In the UK this is the Information Commissioner’s Office. Use the on-device controls for records that only you hold. Material changes will be reflected in a dated policy.
