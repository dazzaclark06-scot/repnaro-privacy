Repnaro privacy policy

25 November 2026 · Android phone and Wear OS companion · com.repnaro.app

This policy covers Repnaro’s fitness and meal-planning apps. The publisher and developer is Repnaro. For app support or privacy requests, including deletion of correspondence you have sent us, email dazzaclark06@gmail.com.

The automatic health and watch features described below apply from version 1.31.3.

Your profile, training and meals

You can enter a name, age, sex, measurements, training goals, schedule, available equipment and dietary preferences. Repnaro uses them on the phone to plan training and meals. It saves workouts, drafts, exercise names, dates, sets, repetitions, loads, rest times, intervals, progression, measurements, nutrition targets, meal plans, swaps, custom meals and shopping lists. The recipe and exercise photographs are supplied with the app. You do not need a Repnaro account.

Health Connect on your phone

On Android 14 or newer, you can give Repnaro permission to read exercise sessions from the previous 30 days. It reads the source app, record identifier, activity type, title when available, and start and end times. These details let Repnaro show workouts, calculate duration and recognise repeat imports. If you also allow access to distance, total calories burned or heart rate, Repnaro adds available measurements from the same source to the workout. It can show average heart rate and calculate running pace from elapsed time. Samsung Health activities, including swims, become available after Samsung Health writes them to Health Connect. Repnaro only reads from Health Connect.

When you open Repnaro. Automatic checking is on by default. Once you grant Exercise permission, Repnaro checks for workouts when you open the phone app or return to it. In Repnaro’s Settings → Health Connect you can turn off automatic checking, use Import workouts manually, and see the time of the last successful sync. Manual workout logging works without Health Connect access.

While Repnaro is closed. Background checks are off by default. On supported devices, you can enable Check periodically in the background and grant Android’s separate background health-read permission. With automatic checking enabled, Repnaro asks Android to check every six hours. Android controls when the check runs and may delay it. Repnaro temporarily saves the results in private storage on your phone and merges them into your history when you next use the app.

You can turn off background checks alone or turn off automatic checking to stop both foreground and background checks. You can also revoke individual permissions in Android’s Health Connect settings. Revoking a permission stops future reads of that data. Copies already imported into Repnaro remain until you remove them.

Your watch and paired phone

Repnaro looks for a single nearby phone or watch running the compatible companion app and can connect automatically. Manual pairing is also available. The paired apps exchange workout libraries, routines, targets, completed sets, timings and available measurements. The watch keeps workout entries, including weights and repetitions. The devices keep pairing information, acknowledgements and pending updates so they can reconnect and deliver saved progress after a disconnection.

Starting a workout on the phone can start health recording on the connected watch. You can also start recording on the watch. When hardware and permissions allow, Wear OS Health Services supplies heart-rate samples and estimated calories. The phone can show a recent live heart rate and save the workout’s available average and maximum heart rate and estimated calories. The watch offers running, cycling and general cardio on supported devices. It records duration and can also record device-reported distance for running and cycling. Repnaro does not request a GPS route.

On the watch, Body Sensors permission allows access to heart rate, and Physical Activity permission supports calorie estimates. You can manage these permissions in the watch’s app settings. Denying a permission can prevent sensor recording. You can still log a workout on the phone without a watch.

Google Play services provides the Wear OS Data Layer. Nearby traffic uses encrypted Bluetooth. When devices use the Google cloud relay, Google documents end-to-end encryption of the message content. Repnaro authenticates paired messages. Device and installation identifiers used for reliable delivery differ from advertising IDs.

Where information goes

Repnaro stores records in Android app-private storage. Android’s device protection applies. Ordinary app records do not have a separate password-encryption layer. Automatic Android app backup is disabled. There is no Repnaro-hosted health database, advertising, account, analytics or crash-reporting service built into these apps.

Portable backups and restore

In Settings → Backup & Recovery you can export a portable backup. Repnaro encrypts it with your recovery password before writing it to the destination you choose in Android’s document picker. That destination can be on the device, removable media or a cloud provider. The provider can see file metadata. Keep the password separately. Repnaro cannot recover it. Backups can include profile, workout, nutrition, measurement and pending recovery records from the phone. Pairing credentials and records present only on a disconnected watch are excluded. Previously created plain JSON backups remain readable by anyone who has them.

Restore verifies the backup and asks for confirmation before replacing supported records. Use the date and data summary to identify the copy you want. The app can retain a local safety copy during recovery. Google Play, Google Play services, your device software, Health Connect, the website host and any file provider you choose operate under their respective privacy policies. Opening the hosted policy sends normal web-request information such as your IP address to the host. Reading the bundled copy inside the app works offline.

Retention, deletion and security

If you email us, the publisher and its email provider receive your address, message and any attachments you send. We use them to handle your request. If you join a private test, your invitation address is used to manage access through Google Play. Please keep health records, backup files and recovery passwords out of support messages. You can ask us to remove your correspondence or remove you from our tester list.

Training records stay on the device until you remove them. In Settings → Data Management → Factory reset Repnaro, confirm RESET to erase Repnaro’s local phone records and safety copies. Handle the watch’s app storage separately. External backup files, provider trash or version history and original records held by Health Connect or Samsung Health each have their own deletion controls. Data already synced can remain on the other device. To remove all Repnaro app data from a device, including local sync settings and pending updates, use that device’s system settings to clear Repnaro’s app storage. Read the full data deletion instructions.

Your choices, contact and changes

For questions about your information, contact the publisher using the route above. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or erase information the publisher holds and to raise a concern with your local data protection authority. In the UK this is the Information Commissioner’s Office. Use the on-device controls for records that only you hold. Material changes will be reflected in a dated policy.
